Backtest on your saved history

Replays this same rule on the candles your bridge saved, day by day, and shows how it would have done. It leaves out spreads and slippage. At 1:2 you need to win more than 33% of trades just to break even, and fewer than 100 trades is too few to trust.

How it works

The first 15 minutes after the New York open (09:30 New York time, which is 15:30 here now and 16:30 from 1 Nov) set a range. The first close outside it gives a Buy above or a Sell below. The stop goes on the other side of the range and take profit is at 1:2. Move the stop to break-even at 50% of the way to take profit. One signal per market per day, and none against the 3-hour trend.

How it works

Each market is scored out of 100: 1-hour trend sets direction (30), 15-minute trend agrees (15), pullback to the 20 EMA holds (20), RSI not stretched (15), close beyond the last 6-bar high or low (10), volatility normal (10). Score 75 or more becomes a signal. A signal stays until its take profit or its stop loss, however long that takes. If the 4-hour trend also agrees and the score is 80 or more, it becomes a swing signal with a wider stop, and a 1:3 target. A second setup on the same pair gets its own card. Signals are saved in this browser. This is a rule-based filter, not a prediction.
